A club for the in-between moments

More than a club.
A place to belong.

Campus Connect started with a simple idea: college is better when we make it together. We’re a welcoming, student-led community for meeting people, learning by doing, and making campus feel a little smaller.

Why we’re here

Connection changes the whole campus experience.

Starting college, changing courses, or just looking for something new can feel like a lot. Our club creates easy ways to show up, join in, and find your people—without needing a perfect plan or a particular background.

Members shape what we do. Some bring a project idea; others bring good questions, a helping hand, or simply themselves. Every contribution counts.
